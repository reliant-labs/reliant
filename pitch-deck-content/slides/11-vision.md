# The engineering layer for a world run by agents

Workflows that outlive the models running them.

---
**Speaker Notes:**
Here's the bet. Models keep changing every few months, and every team keeps rebuilding the same prompts and guardrails from scratch when they do. We think the durable thing isn't the model, it's the workflow — the orchestration and verification a team writes once and keeps. So the picture in five years is a library of those workflows that teams author, share, and improve, sitting on top of whatever model is best that week. That library is the part that gets harder to walk away from the longer you use it. Call it Workflows That Survive.
