The project **Usage** page answers "what is this app costing me?" It covers one app's [hosting](/hosting/overview): the compute, database storage and static-site bandwidth its environments use. It does not cover everything Reliant bills you for, and the difference matters: read [what isn't here](#what-isnt-here) before you add this page up against an invoice.

## Where to find it

Open the project and choose **Usage** under **Configure**. The page is the project's own; the organization-wide view is **Usage & billing** in the footer of the sidebar, which lists each hosted project's month so far and forecast and links back to each project's Usage page. Plans, credits, invoices and payment methods are changed in [Settings → Billing](/account/billing).

## What you see

Three headline figures sit at the top. **This month so far** is the estimated bill for the current UTC calendar month, **Forecast for the month** is where it is heading, with a likely range, and **Last month** is the previous month in full, invoiced when a finalized invoice covers it and estimated otherwise. Below them:

- **By day.** A stacked chart of each day's cost by category (hosted compute, databases, database storage, CDN and bandwidth, static sites, builds), at list price before allowances.
- **By environment and by category.** The same list-price total split two ways, so you can see whether `prod` or a forgotten preview environment is the expensive one.
- **What costs the most.** One row per workload with its environment, category, the capacity it requests (replicas, CPU, memory, storage), its cost and its share. A workload you deleted this month still appears, marked deleted, because it used resources while it ran.

Every figure says how it was reached. **Invoiced** means a finalized invoice covers it. **Estimated** is list price minus this project's share of the organization's allowances, final only when the invoice replaces it. **List** is the plan's price before allowances. An amount Reliant couldn't measure reads **n/a**, never `$0`, and a total that leaves out something real is shown as a lower bound ("≥ $12.40"), such as usage that has no price yet.

## How to turn it on

There is nothing to enable. Deploy an environment to Reliant hosting and the page fills in as usage is measured; before that it says no hosted usage has been recorded this month. The forecast needs about a day: it isn't shown until 24 hours of usage have been measured, and it is built from the trailing seven days. Costs lag slightly, because the page includes usage up to the cost rollup's most recent run rather than to the second.

## What isn't here

Reliant bills several things, and only some of them belong to a project.

| Cost | On this page? |
|---|---|
| Hosted compute (CPU, memory), managed database storage, static-site bandwidth and CDN | Yes, per project and environment |
| AI model usage (Reliant AI credit and your wallet) | No. Billed to the organization, not to a project. |
| Agent sandbox time (cloud machines) | No. A sandbox belongs to a person and may serve many projects. |
| Observability ingestion | No. Counted for the organization, with no project split. |

The page ends with a note saying that AI and agent sandbox time are billed to the organization. Those figures are in [Settings → Billing](/account/billing), and [observability billing](/hosting/observability-billing) explains ingestion. Older docs and parts of the CLI call agent sandboxes "machines". Attributing AI and sandbox cost to a project is not available yet.

## Limits

- Hosted usage only: an environment on your own cluster, host or Docker Compose has nothing for Reliant to meter.
- The numbers are estimates until an invoice finalizes them. Allowances for egress and observability are applied at the organization level and shared out across projects, so one project's estimate can move when another's usage changes.
- Reading the page needs permission to read your organization's billing. If it can't be read, the page says so and points to Settings → Billing instead of showing zeros.
- Caps and budgets apply to the organization. Your hosting agreement's monthly admission budget is described in [Hosting on Reliant](/hosting/overview#billing).
